When exporting wine from South Africa, the following documents and procedures are typically required: Document Purpose Issuing Authority Export Permit An export permit is required from the South African Wine and Spirit Board (WSB) to export wine from South Africa. The permit must be obtained before exporting wine and must be displayed on the shipment. South African Wine and Spirit Board (WSB) Health Certificate A health certificate is required to ensure that the wine is free from harmful contaminants and meets health and safety standards. The health certificate is issued by the South African Department of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries (DAFF). Department of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries (DAFF) Invoice A commercial invoice is required to accompany each shipment and must include detailed information about the wine, including the variety, vintage, and alcohol content. The invoice must also include the names and addresses of the seller, buyer, and consignee. Exporting Company Bill of Lading A bill of lading is a shipping document that serves as a receipt for the wine and a contract for the transportation of the wine. It must include detailed information about the shipment, including the number of bottles, the weight and volume of the shipment, and the shipping route. Shipping Company Phytosanitary Certificate A phytosanitary certificate is required to certify that the wine has been produced and processed in accordance with the international phytosanitary standards. The certificate is issued by the DAFF. DAFF Certificate of Origin A certificate of origin is required to confirm that the wine was produced in South Africa and meets the origin requirements for the importing country. The certificate of origin is usually issued by the Chamber of Commerce. Chamber of Commerce Label Approval In some countries, approval of the label design and packaging is required before exporting wine. This approval must be obtained from the relevant government agency in the importing country. Relevant Government Agency (Importing Country) Customs Declaration A customs declaration must be submitted to the customs authorities in both the exporting and importing countries. The declaration must include detailed information about the wine, including the value, weight, and volume of the shipment.
